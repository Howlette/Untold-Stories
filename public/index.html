The series

One season. One true story.

Each season follows a single true story from start to finish over several episodes. Every story follows a person who met serious obstacles with courage and hard work, and in doing so protected the rights of others or opened doors for the generations that followed. Every season carries the same message. If they did it, so can you.

Each year we select one or two new stories for screenplay development. Each story is based on true events. The key facts stay true, and some scenes, dialogue, and characters are created for drama.

A strong story has

  • Events that really happened
  • Records that back it up
  • Real risk or sacrifice
  • A turning point
  • An impact that lasted
  • A story never fully told

We welcome stories from Black, Hispanic and Latino, Asian American, Native American, and immigrant communities, and from anyone who overcame prejudice or hardship to succeed.

Before you submit

Four steps to share a story

  1. 1

    Write it down

    Write the full story in your own words, at whatever length it takes. Include names, places, and dates where you know them.

  2. 2

    Register your copyright

    Register your full written story with the United States Copyright Office before you submit. Register the story itself, not a summary. We cannot accept a story without a registration number. Registration can take several months, so start early.

    Example registration numberTXu 2-345-678
    Register at copyright.gov
  3. 3

    Gather your proof

    Collect copies of photographs, letters, news clippings, service records, or court papers. Send copies only. Keep your originals.

  4. 4

    Submit the form

    Upload the same full story you registered, enter its registration number, and agree to the submission terms.

What happens next

Our team and our legal counsel review every complete submission. If your story moves forward, we will contact you to discuss a written agreement. If you do not hear from us, your story was not selected this cycle, and you remain free to share it anywhere else.
